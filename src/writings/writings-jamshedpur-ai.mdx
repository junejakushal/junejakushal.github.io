The pattern across the conversations is not that AI is replacing judgment, but that it is being slotted into very specific roles. For some, it is the starting point of the search; for others, a sanity check; for a third group, a source to be deliberately avoided when the stakes involve skin. Trust is still earned elsewhere — through familiar brands, real reviews, dermatologists, or friends.
